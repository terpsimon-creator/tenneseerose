The Final&Rose

♛

Sixteen suitors. One bride. A bracket of beautiful, terrible men. Will she find love, or just a nightmare with a jawline?

How to Play

✦

1 — Setup

Enter the bride's name. Each of the 16 bridesmaids will take turns drawing a bachelor card from the deck. The hotter the bachelor, the worse his flaw. Bridesmaids should write their own name on the back of each card they draw — they'll perform that bachelor for the bride.

2 — The Pitch

One by one, bridesmaids present their bachelor in character. Accents, props, dramatic readings — the works. The bride sees a card on screen and decides who advances.

3 — The Bracket

16 → 8 → 4 → 2 → Winner. Random chaos may strike between rounds: villain edits, confessionals, audience overrides. The Final Four face the Fantasy Suite question round.

4 — The Final Rose

The winner receives the Black Rose. Then comes the Unmasking — flip each card to reveal which bridesmaid was secretly behind which bachelor. Screams. Tears. Cackling.
